01 — How it works

Two pipelines, one dashboard

Reactive

User-reported issues

A campus member submits a photo, a short description, and a location. A CNN fine-tuned on infrastructure images predicts the category in seconds, routes it to the right team, and starts the resolution clock.

Camera-based verification

A morning and evening image pair from the same fixed point is compared with the Structural Similarity Index. A score below threshold confirms the task was actually done — no self-reporting required.
